## Research question

How can medial structures reveal meaningful features of a triangulation while remaining stable and computationally tractable?

## Method

Small synthetic examples act as controlled experiments. Computed axes are cached with provenance and rendered independently from the solver, making comparisons deterministic and inexpensive.

## Software

The medial axis code used here is [the work of Elizabeth Stephenson, Martin Hafskjold Thoresen, and Herbert Edelsbrunner](https://medial-ax.github.io/medial-ax/). Our collaboration builds on that codebase later.

Bouquet2D is [written by Rohit Roy](https://bouquet2d.rohitroy.me/); our collaboration on the related paper and software comes later.
